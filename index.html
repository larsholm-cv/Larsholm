About

Profile

I am a procurement specialist with more than ten years in the building-materials industry, currently responsible for direct materials at Idealcombi A/S — the family-owned window manufacturer based in Horsens, Denmark. My category scope covers insulated glass, aluminium profiles, FSC-certified timber and window hardware.

I work hands-on with frame agreements, supplier scorecards and RFQ processes, and partner with R&D on value engineering across the product range. A growing part of my role is the compliance side of sourcing: FSC chain-of-custody, scope-3 data collection and CBAM documentation for aluminium imports.

My approach is pragmatic and prepared — clear specifications, honest supplier dialogue, and negotiations built on facts rather than pressure.
